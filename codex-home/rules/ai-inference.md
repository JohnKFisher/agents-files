# AI and Inference

Read when adding/changing ranking, classification, summarization, recommendation, generated descriptions/text, or other inferential behavior.

- New inferential product behavior requires explicit approval unless already established by the task/project decisions.
- Keep source facts, deterministic/rule-based logic, AI output, and fallback behavior distinguishable. Do not let inference silently override explicit user choices, configuration, source truth, or approved rules.
- Use factual phrasing and expose material uncertainty. For user-visible generated content, prefer preview/edit/confirm and clear deterministic fallback where practical.
